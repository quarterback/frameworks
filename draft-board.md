# Draft Board Dating
### A constraint-based alternative to extraction dating apps

## The Problem

Dating apps profit from keeping you searching, not from helping you find someone. The longer you stay on the platform, the more valuable you are. This creates a fundamental misalignment: you want to find a partner and leave, but the platform needs you frustrated and engaged.

[Reference the Jacobin article - how platforms monetize desire and disappointment]

The result: infinite choice creates paralysis, attention asymmetry creates frustration, and the apps succeed by failing their users.

## The Alternative: Draft Board Mechanics

What if dating apps worked like fantasy sports instead of slot machines?

**Core Mechanics:**

### 1. Finite Roster (Constraint-Based Selection)
- Limited roster slots (10-15 profiles)
- Forces actual filtering instead of endless browsing
- Can't evaluate everyone—must prioritize

### 2. Draft Ranking (Strategic Allocation)
- Rank your roster across 5 draft rounds
- Round 1: 2 picks (scarcest, highest priority)
- Round 2-5: Descending allocation
- Can't make everyone a first-rounder—must commit to preferences

### 3. Stratified Pools (Market Organization)
- Algorithm groups users by draft position
- First-rounders see first-rounders
- Fifth-rounders see fifth-rounders
- Reduces variance, improves realistic matching

### 4. Progression Structure (Resolution Pressure)
- Draft interviews for mutual matches
- Structured conversation with progress requirements
- Either advance to meeting or release back to pool
- No infinite messaging limbo

## Why This Works

**Distributes attention more evenly** - Can't spray first-round picks everywhere. Women aren't drowning in undifferentiated messages.

**Forces actual decisions** - Limited picks with ranked priority creates real evaluation, not consumption.

**Aligns incentives** - Platform succeeds when people match and leave, not when they stay frustrated and searching.

**Honest economics** - Pay for more draft picks/roster slots, not for algorithmic manipulation or false visibility promises.

**Familiar mechanics** - Everyone understands fantasy drafts. No new interaction patterns to learn.

## Why This Matters

This isn't about building a better dating app. It's about demonstrating how different institutional architecture produces different outcomes.

Current dating platforms are extraction engines—they extract value from your desire for connection while ensuring you never quite find it. Draft board mechanics show an alternative: constraint-based systems that align platform success with user outcomes.

The same principle applies to any matching system where infinite choice creates paralysis and platforms profit from frustration rather than resolution.
